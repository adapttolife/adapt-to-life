Our Story

Built by the community it serves.

Adapt To Life was built by adaptive athletes and coaches, the people who lived the problem, then set out to fix it for everyone behind them.

The team

The people behind the mission.

Athletes and coaches who've lived this. The fund honors one of our own, Tim Gruensfelder.

Leadership Team

Karen Atkinson

An Army captain and disabled veteran who found adaptive sports on the other side of her service. A Move United Warfighter Ambassador, an AMP Adaptive Sports board member, and First Chair of Stand Up and Play. She's the athlete this work is built for, and she keeps us honest.

Board Member

Tom Daily

Coached wheelchair basketball for decades. He didn't just teach athletes to play. He taught them to advocate, to lead, and to take up space in rooms that weren't built for them.

Board Member

Mike Carrico

Retired Director of Racquets at the Birchwood Club and an adaptive tennis coach with two decades on the court. He once coached a kid in a wheelchair he called Hot Shot. That kid grew up to found Adapt To Life.

Leadership Team

Pete Petersen

Came to coach tennis with no background in disability and helped build the wheelchair pickleball program from the ground up. In 2025 he competed in hybrid doubles at the US Open Pickleball Championships in Naples.

The founder

The organization I needed at six, built now by the team around me.

Alec Tranel has been in a wheelchair since he was six; by eighteen, twenty-eight operations. A few months after his injury, a physical therapist handed him a brochure for adaptive sports he didn't know existed. A gym led to a coach named Tom Daily, who saw an athlete, not a kid in a chair. Alec spent more than twenty years in this community as an athlete and a coach, then came back to coach at the program that raised him.

I got lucky. Great coaches, the right equipment, someone who believed in me before I believed in myself. Not everyone gets that lucky. That's the whole reason we're here.

Alec Tranel

Founder & Executive Director
How every dollar works

One hundred percent reaches the athlete.

We don't replace existing programs. We fund the costs their budgets were never built to carry, and we pay them directly, so every dollar goes exactly where it was given.
