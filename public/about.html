Our Story

Built by the community it serves.

We expand access to adaptive sports so athletes can find their place. We are athletes and coaches who know what that place can mean, and we are building the support to help more people find it.

The team

The people behind the mission.

Athletes and coaches with years in this community. Our fund honors Tim Gruensfelder.

Board Member

Karen Atkinson

An Army captain and disabled veteran who found adaptive sports on the other side of her service. A Move United Warfighter Ambassador, an AMP Adaptive Sports board member, and First Chair of Stand Up and Play. She brings an athlete's perspective to the work.

Learn more about Karen
Board Member

Mike Carrico

Retired Director of Racquets at the Birchwood Club and an adaptive tennis coach with two decades on the court. He once coached a kid in a wheelchair he called Hot Shot. That kid grew up to found Adapt To Life.

Leadership Team

Pete Petersen

Came to coach tennis with no background in disability and helped build the wheelchair pickleball program from the ground up. In 2025 he competed in hybrid doubles at the US Open Pickleball Championships in Naples.

The founder

It started with a brochure.

Alec Tranel has been in a wheelchair since he was six; by eighteen, twenty-eight operations. A few months after his injury, a physical therapist handed him a brochure for adaptive sports he didn't know existed. A gym led to a coach who saw an athlete, not a kid in a chair. Alec spent more than twenty years in this community as an athlete and a coach, then came back to coach at the program that raised him.

I got lucky. Great coaches, the right equipment, someone who believed in me before I believed in myself. Not everyone gets that lucky. That's the whole reason we're here.

Alec Tranel

Founder & Executive Director
Our work

We build more ways into adaptive sports.

There are people doing good work in adaptive sports all over the country. We work alongside them, helping people find each other and building resources that make it easier to take part.

Adaptive Sports Near Me

Our free directory brings programs together so athletes, families, and people making a referral can find somewhere to start. We are building on that foundation to make useful information easier to find and share.

The Hustle & Heart Fund

Finding an opportunity does not always mean being able to afford it. The fund helps with equipment, training, and travel so athletes can take the next step in a sport they want to play.

Coming soon

Adapt Body Shop

Our upcoming store will focus on gear and apparel for the adaptive sports community. We are developing it to serve the people who play and help sustain the work around them.

These are starting points, not the limits of the mission. What we build next should come from what the community needs.
