How we grow together ↓
Why ACE

Every athlete deserves an open court. ACE opened its doors.

13 courts
Indoor and cushioned, with one reserved for the crew every week.
Members
Who play side by side with the athletes in open play.
Coaches
Who make players better and meet athletes where they are, ambulatory or in a wheelchair.

ACE stood with adaptive pickleball in August, months before the crew’s first night. In November, the crew took the court.

How we grow together

We grow when ACE grows.

ACE already gives a portion of every drop-in back to the athletes. We want our growth to add to ACE, not cost it.

  1. Now

    Open the door

    ACE makes room on its courts, and the athletes play on court time we fund.

  2. Coming up

    Build support

    Sponsors and partners join in.

    • A member fundraiser with ACE
    • Try Adaptive Night: ACE members play from sport chairs alongside the crew
    • The Chicago Classic, a wheelchair pickleball tournament at ACE. Date to come.
  3. Where we’re headed

    Full price

    ACE reaches its regular price, and drop-ins grow into members.

    An idea we’re exploring with ACE: memberships for athletes training to compete.

  4. The club sets the pace.

For ACE

  • Paid court time, working toward the club’s regular price
  • New faces at open play
  • Drop-ins who grow into members
  • A story that sets the club apart

For the athletes

  • Courts that welcome them
  • Open play with members
  • A place to train for bigger tournaments
