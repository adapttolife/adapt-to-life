Where we start

You were with us early.

This partnership did not start with a pitch. It started with ACE making room for us.

  1. August 2026ACE Des Plaines posted that it is proud to support adaptive pickleball.
  2. August 22 and 23You gave us an info table, and room for a sport chair, at your tournament.
  3. The recapOur video from your tournament drew about 3,000 views across Instagram and Facebook.
  4. NowACE is our weekly home on Thursdays. Everything below builds from here.

The wishlist

Everything we'd love to explore.

Not a contract and not a to-do list. These are ideas to talk through. We pick what fits ACE, start small, and build on what works.

01

Play every week

  • Thursdays at ACE. A set time and group size that works for your members.
  • Daytime sessions as we grow. Midday is when courts open up, and your Mid-Day membership already fits it.
  • Highlights from your court cameras. Clips from every session that we can both share.

02

Open the doors

  • Try Adaptive Pickleball Night. ACE members play from sport chairs alongside our athletes.
  • An adaptive session in your Starter Series. Our athletes help teach the game the way they play it.
  • Member hitting partners. ACE members who become regular partners for our players.
  • Equipment demo day. Mobility businesses we know bring sport chairs to try.

03

Tell the story

  • A collab post from every session, so it lands on your feed and ours.
  • ACE on our website, at our events, and in our fundraisers.
  • Our story on your club screens.
  • Local press together. A first for adaptive pickleball in Chicago is a story worth telling.

04

Compete together

  • An adaptive exhibition at the Championship Series. The series has no adaptive division yet. ACE Des Plaines could be first.
  • Des Plaines vs Vernon Hills, adaptive edition. Your club rivalry, with an adaptive match on the card.
  • Illinois' first sanctioned wheelchair tournament. USA Pickleball runs wheelchair divisions at Nationals. We found none in Illinois yet.

05

Give together

  • Sponsor an athlete. Members or local businesses cover an athlete's ACE membership.
  • A Glow Night fundraiser. Proceeds help send six of our athletes to the US Open in spring 2027.
  • Our athletes at your events. Volunteering at check-in, clinics, and tournaments.

06

Grow together

  • The biggest adaptive pickleball program in Chicago, with ACE as home base.
  • A model for other ACE clubs. When it works here, we share the playbook.
What success looks like

A win for ACE. A win for our athletes.

We want to agree on what a great year looks like before we start, then check in against it together.

For our athletes

  • A reliable weekly home they can count on
  • More people trying adaptive pickleball for the first time
  • Athletes improving and competing at bigger tournaments

For ACE

  • New members and drop-ins who came through us
  • Members taking part, as hitting partners, at try-it nights, and as fans
  • A community story that sets ACE Des Plaines apart

For both of us

  • Events we host together
  • Dollars raised for athletes
  • A partnership other clubs want to copy

How we keep score

Every month we send you a one-page recap: attendance, new players, new ACE members and drop-ins, views on our ACE posts, events held, and dollars raised. We set the targets together, and we check in every quarter.
