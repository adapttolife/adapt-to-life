You can't play what you can't find.

Too many people never find adaptive sports, because no one tells them it's there. A brochure in the right hands changed everything for our founder. This is that brochure at scale: every program we can find, in one place, open to anyone looking.

No gatekeeping, no paywall. If a program serves adaptive athletes, it belongs here, and an athlete should find it in seconds, not after months of asking around.

A program in the directory

AMP Adaptive Sports

Elkhorn, Wisconsin

AMP gets athletes out on the water, water skiing on a lake most people just drive past. Local, specific, and life-changing for whoever finds it. Multiply that by every town with a program nobody knows about, and you understand why this has to exist.

We didn't just put AMP in the directory. We got in the water.
