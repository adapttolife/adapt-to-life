You can't play what you can't find.

A brochure introduced our founder to adaptive sports. Adaptive Sports Near Me makes that first step easier for the next person, bringing existing programs together in one place. It can help an athlete explore a new sport, a family find a first program, or a clinician make a referral.

The directory is a starting point, not a guarantee of availability. Contact the program to confirm schedules, eligibility, equipment, and accessibility.
