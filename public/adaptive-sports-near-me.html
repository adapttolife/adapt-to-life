Adaptive Sports Near Me

Find a program near you

A free, open directory of adaptive sports programs across the country. Search by where you live and what you want to play. No account, no cost, no catch.

Visit the directory
Why we built it

You can't play what you can't find

Most people never find adaptive sports because no one tells them it exists. A brochure in the right hands changed our founder's life. The directory is that brochure at scale: every program we can find, in one place, open to anyone looking.

There is no gatekeeping and no paywall. If a program serves adaptive athletes, it belongs in the directory, and an athlete should be able to find it in seconds, not after months of asking around.

How it works

Built to be used

Search by location

Find programs near where you live, not three states away. Start with your area and go from there.

Filter by sport

Wheelchair basketball, tennis, pickleball, skiing, cycling, and more. Search for what you actually want to play.

Free and open

No account, no fee, no login wall. The directory exists to connect athletes to programs, full stop.

A program in the directory

AMP Adaptive Sports

Elkhorn, Wisconsin

AMP Adaptive Sports gets athletes out on the water, water skiing on a lake most people just drive past. It is exactly the kind of program the directory exists to surface: local, specific, and life changing for the people who find it. Multiply that by every town with a program nobody knows about, and you understand why this needs to exist.
