What ambassadors do

More than a following.

01

Athlete ambassador

Welcome newcomers, show the sport, share the parts of your story you choose, and represent the community at events.

02

Coach or program builder

Lead sessions within your experience, support local staff, and help a host keep the sport going after the first day.

03

Community connector

Make the introductions that matter: schools, families, clinics, and programs, so people find a place to play.

These are ways in, not ranks. Nobody has to do all three.

How it works

A real role, with real support.

What you get

  • A clear role and a named contact.
  • Travel and access support for the work we ask you to do.
  • Training, materials, and introductions.
  • Your own page on this site, if you want one.

What we ask

  • Show up for what you agree to.
  • Represent the community honestly.
  • Tell us about any paid brand relationships, so we can tell everyone else.

Being an ambassador never depends on receiving support from the Hustle & Heart Fund, and support never depends on being an ambassador.
