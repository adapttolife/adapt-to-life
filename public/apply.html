The Hustle & Heart Fund

Grant application.

Funding for the equipment, travel, and training that get adaptive athletes in the game. Tell us what's standing between you and your sport.

Already in the game Athletes the fund has already put back on a court.
Who this is for

Adaptive athletes of any age, sport, or level. If the cost of competing is the thing keeping you out, you're exactly who the Hustle & Heart Fund is built for. No referral, no red tape.

Where the fund stands today. The Hustle & Heart Fund is new and the money in it is small. Applying is not a promise of a grant. It is how we learn who needs help and what it costs, and it puts you in front of us when the money is there. We would rather say that plainly than leave you waiting on something we never promised.

We read every application, wherever you are. Apply even if you're not sure you qualify.

The fund covers equipment, travel, and training, the real costs of competing. How the fund works →

Is this you?
  • An adaptive athlete, any age, sport, or level
  • Cost is the thing keeping you out
  • Ready to use the gear, the trip, or the training

We don't fund everyday medical or mobility gear, or costs another program already covers. Not sure you fit? Apply anyway and ask.

What happens next

1 · Apply. A few minutes, that's it.

2 · Review. A person reads it. We confirm the need and the real cost.

3 · Decision. Some we can fund now. Some wait for the fund to catch up. You hear either way.

4 · Covered. Approved grants are paid straight to the vendor, so you never handle the money.

Want it to move faster?

The fund grows fastest through people who already care about one athlete. If you want to help it along, share it with the people who root for you. Everything raised goes back out as grants. This is entirely optional and it has no bearing on your application.

Share the fund →

One hundred percent reaches the athlete. We'll email you a receipt now, and we'll tell you where things stand even when the answer is not yet.
