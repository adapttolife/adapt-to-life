The Hustle & Heart Fund

Grant application.

Need help with equipment, training, or travel? Tell us what you want to do and what the cost is keeping you from doing.

Who this is for

Any age, sport, or level. You do not need a referral or a competition record.

Grants depend on the need, the cost, and the funds available. Applying does not guarantee an award.

We read every application, wherever you are. Apply even if you're not sure you qualify.

The fund supports equipment, training, and travel for adaptive sports. How the fund works

Is this you?
  • An adaptive athlete, any age, sport, or level
  • Cost is limiting your participation
  • A specific equipment, training, or travel need

We don't fund everyday medical or mobility gear, or costs another program already covers. Not sure you fit? Apply anyway and ask.

What happens next

1 · Apply. Tell us about your sport and the support you need.

2 · Review. A person reads it. We confirm the need and the real cost.

3 · Decision. We consider the request and available funds, then follow up by email.

4 · Funding. For approved grants, we arrange a direct payment or reimbursement with you.

No fundraising requirement.

You do not have to donate, fundraise, or share the fund to apply. If you choose to share it, that is entirely optional and has no bearing on your application.

Share the fund

Use an email address you check. We will send an application confirmation and follow up there.

Common questions

Before you apply.

Can I apply for an expense I have already paid?

Yes. Tell us what you paid for and when. We consider past expenses case by case.

How much can I get?

Grant amounts depend on your request, eligibility, and available funds.

Where do you fund athletes?

We welcome applications from adaptive athletes wherever they live.

How soon will I hear back?

Review times vary. We follow up at the email you provide.
