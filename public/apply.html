The Hustle & Heart Fund

Grant application.

Funding for the equipment, travel, and training that get adaptive athletes in the game. Tell us what's standing between you and your sport.

Who this is for

Adaptive athletes of any age, sport, or level. If the cost of competing is the thing keeping you out, you're exactly who the Hustle & Heart Fund is built for. No referral, no red tape.

We're building the fund now, so applying puts you first in line. Every application tells us who needs help and what it costs, so the moment we can fund, we know right where to start.

What it covers

Equipment. Sport chairs, racing gear, protective gear, repairs.

Travel. Entry fees, flights, lodging, and meals for competition.

Training. Coaching, clinics, and gym time.

What happens next

1 · Apply. A few minutes, that's it.

2 · Review. We confirm the need and the real costs.

3 · Approve. Approved grants are funded directly.

4 · Covered. We pay the cost; you get on the court.

100% of the fund goes to athletes. We'll follow up at the email you provide.
