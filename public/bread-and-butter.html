Juan’s Bread & Butter code

Play Juan’s paddle. Help get him to Naples.

Juan Ortiz plays a Bread & Butter Loco. His code takes 10% off at Bread & Butter, and the commission they pay him goes to Adapt To Life for his road to the US Open.

His code JuanitoLoco
Shop Bread & Butter See the lineup

Juan is a Bread & Butter ambassador. Bread & Butter pays him a commission on orders that use his code, and he gives it to Adapt To Life.

How it works

Shop, add the code, and it counts.

  1. 01

    Shop Bread & Butter

    Pick a paddle or gear at bnbpickleball.com. Bread & Butter handles the order, shipping and returns.

  2. 02

    Enter JuanitoLoco

    Add his code at checkout for 10% off. The code is how Bread & Butter knows the order came from Juan.

  3. 03

    The commission comes to the fund

    Juan gives his commission to Adapt To Life, for the Hustle & Heart Fund and his road to the US Open.

Without the code, the order does not count for Juan. Current prices, stock and shipping are on Bread & Butter’s site.

His paddle

The Loco.

“It’s highly rated, super user friendly, and plays with unbelievable pop and control. The moment I tried it, I was sold.”

Juan plays the Loco from his PER4MAX sport chair, with the Adapt To Life QR code on the frame. Bread & Butter’s new Loco 2 comes in the same three shapes.

“If you see me out on the court, come try my Loco yourself.”

Where it goes

On the road to his first US Open.

Juan holds the first of six seats on The Road to the US Open: tournaments through the year for six wheelchair pickleball athletes, then Naples in spring 2027. About $3,500 gets Juan there if his name comes up in the lottery.

Every gift goes to the Hustle & Heart Fund for Chicago Pickleball: Wheelie Big Dills, every week first, then the road to the US Open.

Questions

The short answers.

What is Juan’s Bread & Butter code?

JuanitoLoco. Enter it at checkout on bnbpickleball.com for 10% off.

Where does the money go?

Bread & Butter pays Juan a commission on orders that use his code, and Juan gives it to Adapt To Life. It goes to the Hustle & Heart Fund, toward his road to the US Open in Naples.

Is this a donation?

No. It is a purchase from Bread & Butter, so it is not tax deductible. To give directly, use the Donate button. Gifts to Adapt To Life, a 501(c)(3), are tax deductible to the extent allowed by law.

Who is Juan Ortiz?

Juan is a Wheelchair Softball Hall of Famer, a dad of three, and Adapt To Life’s first ambassador. He is building wheelchair pickleball in Chicago and chasing his first US Open.
