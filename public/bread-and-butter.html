How it works

Shop, add the code, and it counts.

  1. 01

    Shop Bread & Butter

    Pick a paddle or gear at bnbpickleball.com. Bread & Butter handles the order, shipping and returns.

  2. 02

    Enter JuanitoLoco

    Add his code at checkout for 10% off. The code is how Bread & Butter knows the order came from Juan.

  3. 03

    The commission comes to the fund

    Juan gives his commission to Adapt To Life’s Hustle & Heart Fund, which keeps his crew on court every week, then on the road to the US Open.

About Bread & Butter

A family company, now part of Selkirk.

The Sapusek family runs Bread & Butter Pickleball Co., the maker of the Loco, the Filth and the Psycho. In May 2026 it joined Selkirk Sport and kept its own brand, website and paddles.

Where it goes

On the road with his crew.

The Road to the US Open is a season of tournament travel for Chicago Pickleball: Wheelie Big Dills, ending at the US Open in Naples in spring 2027. The US Open picks players by lottery, so nothing is guaranteed.

Every gift goes to the Hustle & Heart Fund for the Wheelie Big Dills, every week first, then the road to the US Open.

Questions

The short answers.

What is Juan’s Bread & Butter code?

JuanitoLoco. Enter it at checkout on bnbpickleball.com for 10% off.

Where does the money go?

Bread & Butter pays Juan a commission on orders that use his code, and Juan gives it to Adapt To Life. It goes to the Hustle & Heart Fund, which keeps his crew on court every week, then on the road to the US Open.

Is this a donation?

No. It is a purchase from Bread & Butter, so it is not tax deductible. To give directly, use the Donate button. Gifts to Adapt To Life, a 501(c)(3), are tax deductible to the extent allowed by law.

Who on the crew plays the Loco?

Juan Ortiz, Bob Swanson and Robby Wolf. Juan is a Bread & Butter ambassador, and his code is JuanitoLoco.

Can I try a Loco before I buy one?

Juan says it best: “If you see me out on the court, come try my Loco yourself.”

Is Bread & Butter part of Selkirk?

Yes. Selkirk Sport acquired Bread & Butter in May 2026, and Bread & Butter kept its own brand, website and paddles.

Who is Juan Ortiz?

Juan is a Wheelchair Softball Hall of Famer, a dad of three, and Adapt To Life’s first ambassador. He is building wheelchair pickleball in Chicago and chasing his first US Open.
