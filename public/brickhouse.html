Two new starts. One court.

Brick House Pickleball is the first partner of Adapt To Life. They are opening their doors in Lake Forest under new ownership. We are a young nonprofit finding our footing. We would rather start together.

See the grand opening Why this partnership
Our first partner

A place to play is where it starts.

Every athlete we support needs somewhere to play. A club that welcomes adaptive athletes changes what is possible for the people around it: a first rally, a regular game, a reason to come back next week.

Brick House has just finished a full renovation. Wheelchair pickleball is where our support for athletes began. This partnership puts the two side by side, in public, from the first day the new courts open.

What we build together will grow from here. Today, we are getting started.
