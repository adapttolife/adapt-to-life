Brick House and Adapt To Life

Every week. Every season.

Our athletes have trained at Brick House for years. A home court changes what is possible. More people get to try pickleball for the first time, and the athletes chasing bigger goals get the practice it takes to get really good.

What it takes to reach the US Open

  1. Train every weekAt Brick House, all four seasons.
  2. Travel to tournamentsEntry fees, flights, and a place to stay, to earn a ranking.
  3. The US OpenNaples, spring 2027. Our goal is to send six.
