One system: L1 dark, L3 light.

The same component in two themes, built from tokens the way T3 Code themes its app. Only Laura’s colors, in the Adaptive Sports Near Me balance. What we took from T3 Code:

  • Hairlines, not fills. Tiles and cards separate with a 1px border and a whisper of lift, so nothing looks muddy.
  • One radius scale (6, 8, 12, 16px) so every corner belongs to the same family.
  • Tinted badges: “Raising now,” “First priority” and the road’s live percentage, colored at 14–20% with text mixed toward the type color so they stay readable.
  • A button that feels pressable: a 1px top highlight, a shadow tinted with its own orange, and a small press when tapped.
  • Numbers that line up (tabular figures) and one motion curve for the count-up, the bars and the press.

  • Beige #EFE8DC · L3 canvas
  • Off-white #F9F7F2 · L1 card, L3 tiles, type on slate
  • Orange #FF5C39 / #C63F22 · Donate, the money, the road bar
  • Teal #00B7BD · every-week bar, status, highlights on slate
  • Yellow #F6BE00 · the 2px win mark under each stat
