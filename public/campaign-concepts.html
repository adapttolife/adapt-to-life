Laura’s palette, finally used.

Laura’s locked palette already has the colors we’ve been missing. The site only ever used her orange, off-white and beige. Her black is a soft slate, not true black, and her teal, yellow and purple were never used. Each option below uses only her colors, with the balance from the Adaptive Sports Near Me guide: mostly neutrals, orange as the action signal, and one small accent.

  • Black#3B3B46 · Laura's slate: dark sections, text
  • Orange#FF5C39 · action: Donate, the money
  • Teal#00B7BD · data accent
  • Yellow#F6BE00 · wins, celebration
  • Purple#483698 · deep sections
  • Off-white#F9F7F2 · cards
  • Beige#EFE8DC · the page
  • Balance: 70–80% neutrals, 15–25% slate, 5–10% orange (Donate and the money), 0–5% accent.
  • Every bar color was checked for color blindness and contrast. Teal next to orange passes; purple uses a lighter step of Laura’s purple in the bar.
