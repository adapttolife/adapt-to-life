Less orange: three wider palettes.

Same layout you liked, same live numbers. Each palette adds a second dark and a new highlight color. Orange is kept for two things only: the money and the Donate button.

  • Every bar color pair was checked for color blindness and contrast. Pickle green next to orange failed, so green is only a highlight, never a bar.
  • The every-week bar takes each palette’s blue; the road stays orange.
