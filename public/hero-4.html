Our work

How we make more of it.

Adaptive sport is scarce. It costs too much, there is too little of it, and what exists is hard to find. None of that is a fact of life. It's a list of problems, and we're working down it.

  • Afford it

    Adaptive gear is brutally expensive, and a racing chair can cost more than a car. The Hustle & Heart Fund covers equipment, training, and travel, so money is never the reason someone stops.

    How the fund works →
  • Find it

    You can't play what you can't find. Adaptive Sports Near Me is a free, open directory of adaptive programs nationwide, with no logins and no paywalls. It opens soon.

    How the directory works →

    Why "Hustle & Heart"

    Hustle & Heart is how Tim played.

    Tim was the hardest-working player in every gym he rolled into. Cancer changed his body, never what drove him. He set the standard for how the game should be played, and he's the reason this fund exists.
