Your Place inAdaptive Sports.

Why we’re here

A chance to play should not come down to luck.

A brochure introduced our founder, Alec, to adaptive sports when he was six. Sport gave him somewhere to belong and something to work toward. He went on to become a coach in the community that helped raise him.

That first introduction should not depend on luck. Neither should having the support to keep playing. Adapt To Life is built by people who know what that opportunity can mean.

The work keeps growing

We build more ways into adaptive sports.

The community is already here. We help clubs, organizations and coaches build and manage adaptive sports programs, and we fund the athletes who play in them. What we build will keep evolving with the community. The mission stays the same.

The more we grow, the more we give.

We are developing revenue and partnerships that help us keep building useful resources and supporting athletes, beyond the next fundraiser.

Our upcoming store, Adapt Body Shop, is part of that work. Gear for the community, and another way to help sustain what we’re building.
