The cost of playing

The athletes we meet don't quit because they lose interest. They quit because they can't afford to stay in.

None of it is luxury. It's the price of showing up, and the drive is already there. Here's the gap we close.

What the fund is for

Built for one thing: getting adaptive athletes into their sport.

That focus is what lets us say yes fast. If you're ever unsure whether your need fits, apply anyway and ask. We would rather hear from you.

What we fund
  • Sport equipment, from racing chairs to protective gear and repairs
  • Travel to compete: entry fees, flights, hotels, meals
  • Training that sharpens you: coaching, clinics, gym time
What we don't
  • Everyday medical or mobility gear that isn't tied to a sport
  • Costs another program or insurance already covers
  • Gear you've already bought, so apply before you buy
Common questions

Before you apply.

Who can apply?

Any adaptive athlete, any age, any sport, any level. If cost is what's keeping you out, this is for you.

Do I need a referral or paperwork?

No referral and no red tape to apply. If we move forward together, we'll confirm the real cost with a quote before anything is funded.

What can the fund cover?

Equipment, travel, and training. Sport chairs and racing chairs, entry fees and flights, coaching and clinics. The real costs of competing.

How does the money get paid?

We pay the vendor or program directly, so you never front the cost or handle the funds. We don't reimburse gear you've already bought, so apply before you buy.

How much can I get?

It depends on the need and where the fund is that month. We're early and growing, so we fund what we can and we stay honest about it.

Where do you fund athletes?

Anywhere. If you're an adaptive athlete and cost is the barrier, where you live isn't. We read every application, wherever you are, and our directory of adaptive sports reaches nationwide.

How soon will I hear back?

We follow up at the email you give us. We're a growing fund, so timing varies, but every application is read.
