The Hustle & Heart Fund

Keep your game going.

The Hustle & Heart Fund helps adaptive athletes with equipment, training, and travel. Named for Tim Gruensfelder: all hustle, all heart.

Donate Apply for a grant

Tim's love of the game is part of everything this fund stands for. His story →

The cost of playing

The desire to play is there. The cost can stand in the way.

The right equipment gets you started. Training helps you grow. Travel gets you to the next opportunity.

Get equipped

Equipment

Sport chairs, protective gear, and repairs. Equipment that works for the athlete and the sport.

Build skills

Training

Coaching, clinics, and practice space. Time to build skills and confidence, at your own level.

Get there

Travel

Entry fees, transport, lodging, and meals. The costs of getting to an event and taking part.

"A year ago I couldn't get through a day without my cane. Now I can play pickleball, keep up with my kid, and choose how I move. The chair didn't fix me. It gave me options I didn't have before."
Brian Liss Adaptive Athlete

Support for your next step.

A first sport, a regular game, or a competition you have been working toward. The next step is yours. We can pay a vendor or program directly, or reimburse an approved expense. How Giving Works →

Why it carries his name

Tim Gruensfelder, 1996–2018.

Tim found wheelchair basketball after childhood cancer. He chased loose balls, put in the work, and earned a college scholarship. When cancer returned in 2018, he was 22.

The fund carries his name so more athletes can have the chance to play, practice, and grow in a sport they love. Tim's story →
