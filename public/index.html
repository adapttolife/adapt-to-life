The face behind it

This should have existed a long time ago.

I have been in a wheelchair since I was six. By eighteen, twenty eight operations. A few months after my injury, a physical therapist handed me a brochure for adaptive sports. I did not know they existed. I did not even know to ask. That changed everything. I have spent more than twenty years in this community since, as an athlete and a coach, and so have the people who built this with me. Adapt To Life is not one person. It is a team of athletes, coaches, and people who have lived this, who came together to build what should have existed a long time ago.

Alec Tranel
Founder & Executive Director

Why we exist

Access to sport is a right, not a privilege.

The barriers are not scarcity. They are a chair nobody could afford, a trip nobody funded, a gym membership that ran out. That makes them solvable. Our vision is simple. No adaptive athlete sits out because of money, and the path from injury to the playing field is clear, funded, and walked next to people who have been there.

What we do

Two initiatives. One mission.

Removing the money barrier

Hustle & Heart Fund

Adaptive equipment is brutally expensive. A racing chair or a sit ski can cost more than a car. We give direct grants so money is not the reason an athlete stops.
