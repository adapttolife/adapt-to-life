Where we start

We clear the path into the game, one barrier at a time.

A chair nobody could afford. A trip nobody funded. A program nobody could find. Solvable, all of it. We start with the two barriers that stop people first.

The cost barrier

Hustle & Heart Fund

Adaptive gear is brutally expensive. A racing chair can cost more than a car. We help cover those costs so money is never the reason someone stops.
