Access to sport is a right, not a privilege.

The barriers are not scarcity. They are a chair nobody could afford, a trip nobody funded, a gym membership that ran out. That makes them solvable. No adaptive athlete sits out because of money, and the path from injury to the playing field is clear, funded, and walked next to people who have been there.

Why "Hustle & Heart"

The fund carries Tim Gruensfelder's name.

Tim was the hardest working player in every gym he rolled into. Cancer changed his body, but never what drove him. He set the standard for how the game should be played, and he is the reason this fund exists. When we talk about the community we serve, Tim is who we mean.

Read Tim's story →
"A year ago I couldn't get through a day without my cane. Now I can play pickleball, keep up with my kid, and choose how I move. The chair didn't fix me. It gave me options I didn't have before."
Brian Liss Adaptive Athlete

How we are built

The more we grow, the more we give.

Adapt To Life is not one person. It is a team of athletes, coaches, and people who have lived this, who came together to build what should have existed a long time ago. We are building it so that one hundred percent of every dollar given to the fund reaches an athlete. Our operating costs are covered separately, on purpose, so giving is never reduced by overhead. We are early, and we are building the infrastructure to do this at scale.

I got into this because someone handed me a brochure when I was six. Now it is our turn to be that for the next athlete.Alec TranelFounder & Executive Director
