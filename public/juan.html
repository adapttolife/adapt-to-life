Who he is

Push. Play. Persist.

That line is printed on his team shirt, and it is a fair description of how he plays. Juan trains with the Chicago wheelchair pickleball program, on the court Adapt To Life helps pay for, most weeks of the year.

We put his photograph on the front of this website before we ever gave him a page of his own. This is us fixing that.

What the game gave him.

Most people find adaptive sport by accident. Somebody hands them a flyer, or drags them to a gym, and then a whole part of their life opens up that nobody had mentioned was available.

Why there is a donate button on this page

A seat is one athlete.

Adapt To Life is raising $21,000 to send six adaptive pickleball athletes to the US Open in Naples in the spring of 2027. Not a hardship discount. Covered.

  • Equipment Tires that survive tournament surfaces, and a paddle that does not crack on day two. $450
  • Training Court time and coaching all year, not just the week before the flight. $500
  • Travel Airfare, six nights, entry fees, and accessible ground transport in Naples. $2,300

About $3,500 puts one athlete on that court.

These are our own numbers from the last trip and the published entry fees, not estimates. If the campaign is funded, or it cannot go ahead, the money moves to the Hustle & Heart Fund. It stays in adaptive sport either way.
