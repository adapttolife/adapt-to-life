Board Member

Karen Atkinson

Retired Army captain. Twenty years in the Army Reserve, from hospital administrator to JAG officer. Since a 2014 car accident and a spinal cord injury, she has golfed standing up, skied, sailed and learned to dive, and she brings other veterans along.

  • Move United Warfighter Ambassador
  • First Chair, Stand Up and Play Foundation
  • AMP Adaptive Sports board
Read her story In the news
“Programs like these change my life. Normally, my life ends at the pavement.”Karen, on 29 News WVIR, February 2025
Her story

Off the pavement.

  1. The officer

    Twenty years in uniform

    Karen joined the Army Reserve at 17, while she was still in high school. The Army took her through college and law school: first as a hospital administrator, then as a JAG officer helping Guard and Reserve soldiers get their lives in order before they deployed. She deployed herself during Desert Storm, working in a hospital caring for soldiers. She retired as a captain.

  2. 2014

    The accident

    After she retired, a driver swerved into her lane. Her car hit gravel on the shoulder and rolled. The crash left her with a spinal cord injury, and today she uses a wheelchair. Winters in Iowa got harder, so she moved to Florida, and found the water.

  3. The water

    Sailing, diving, skiing

    In Florida she found Warrior Sailing. In 2022 she trained as an adaptive scuba diver with the Dive Pirates Foundation, with her son Tanner as her dive buddy. She has water skied with AMP Adaptive Sports.

  4. Standing up

    Golf, in a VertaCat

    A VertaCat from the Stand Up and Play Foundation lifts her into a standing position to take a full swing. She went from receiving one to sitting first on the foundation’s board, and she is helping build its chapter in the Tampa Bay area.

  5. The snow

    Bringing veterans along

    As a Move United Warfighter Ambassador she skis at camps and clinics built for veterans: The Hartford Ski Spectacular in Breckenridge, Wounded Warriors Weekend with Wintergreen Adaptive Sports in Virginia, and Veterans No Boundaries with Maine Adaptive at Sunday River. She also started Vet Going, a Facebook group where veterans share adventure therapy they can try.

Where she serves

Where Karen serves.

  • First Chair of the board Stand Up and Play Foundation A national, all-volunteer nonprofit that puts people who use wheelchairs back on the golf course, standing up, in a VertaCat. Karen received hers at the foundation’s awards dinner, and she is helping build its new chapter in the Tampa Bay area. standupandplayfoundation.org
  • Warfighter Ambassador Move United Move United’s Warfighter Ambassadors help veterans with disabilities find out how and where to play adaptive sports. moveunitedsport.org
  • Board member AMP Adaptive Sports Our partner nonprofit in Elkhorn, Wisconsin, getting athletes of all abilities on the water and the snow. Karen has water skied with AMP. Our partnership with AMP
  • Board member Adapt To Life Karen helps people find their sport through Adaptive Sports Near Me, the free directory we’re building, and signs up its beta testers. Adaptive Sports Near Me

What she’s building

Helping people find their sport.

Karen helps connect people with programs through Adaptive Sports Near Me. The free directory gives athletes, families, and referral partners somewhere to begin. It is in beta now, and Karen is signing up the testers.
