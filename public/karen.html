Leadership Team

Karen Atkinson

Army captain. Disabled veteran. Lifelong competitor. Now she's helping others find their way into the sports that gave her a second start.

Adapt To Life

Her story

She knows the way in, because she found it herself.

Karen served as a captain in the U.S. Army. She came to adaptive sports on the other side of that service, and found what so many athletes find: not just a way to stay active, but a community, an identity, and a reason to compete again.

Today she is a Move United Warfighter Ambassador, a board member with AMP Adaptive Sports, and First Chair of Stand Up and Play. She has spent years holding doors open for other athletes, and she knows how much it matters to have someone point the way.

At Adapt To Life she sits on the leadership team and keeps the work honest. She is the athlete this was built for.

What she's building

Helping people find their sport.

The hardest part is often just knowing where to start. Karen is helping connect athletes to programs near them through Adaptive Sports Near Me, a free national directory, and helping grow the network of people who guide others in, so no one has to find their way alone.
