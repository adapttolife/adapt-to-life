Karen Atkinson

Army captain, disabled veteran, and adaptive athlete. Karen helps others find the programs and people that make sport part of their lives.

Adapt To Life

Her story

An athlete helping others find their way.

Karen served as a captain in the U.S. Army. Through adaptive sports, she found opportunities to stay active, compete, and connect with other athletes.

Today she is a Move United Warfighter Ambassador, a board member with AMP Adaptive Sports, and First Chair of Stand Up and Play. She has spent years holding doors open for other athletes, and she knows how much it matters to have someone point the way.

She brings that experience to Adapt To Life's leadership team and to the people looking for a place to start.

What she's building

Helping people find their sport.

Karen helps connect people with programs through Adaptive Sports Near Me. The free directory gives athletes, families, and referral partners somewhere to begin. It is in beta now, and Karen is signing up the testers.
