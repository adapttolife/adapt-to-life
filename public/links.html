How wheelchair pickleball works
  • Same court, same net, same paddle as everyone else.
  • The ball may bounce twice before a wheelchair player returns it, and the second bounce can land anywhere.
  • Players stay seated when they hit, and serve with their rear wheels behind the baseline.
