Adaptive sports cost more at every step. Here is every number in our goal for the year ahead, and what each one pays for.

The goal for the year ahead

$50,000, line by line.

For the year ahead, our goal for Chicago Pickleball: Wheelie Big Dills covers a night on court every week for 10 to 15 athletes, a year of memberships for 10 of them, and a season of tournament travel ending at the US Open in Naples in spring 2027. One goal, funded in this order: every new gift goes to the first line until it is covered, and gifts past the road go toward the Chicago Classic.

  1. 01

    Every week

    $27,180 a year

    A year of court time for 10 to 15 athletes a night, plus memberships for 10, so they can play every week and train to compete. Every new gift starts here.

Not in the goal

A sport chair: $4,000 to $6,000.

Insurance doesn’t cover sport wheelchairs. Most of the athletes we support have one. When one needs a chair, we raise for that athlete, outside this goal.
