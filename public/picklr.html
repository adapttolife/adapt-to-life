Our partner club in Mount Prospect

Open play, open doors.

The Picklr in Mount Prospect is our partner club for open play with the Chicago Wheelie Big Dills, right alongside the club’s own players.

How it started

One open house. One email.

This partnership started with the crew showing up to play.

  1. October 4, 2026The crew plays at The Picklr’s open house in Mount Prospect, and the regulars stop to watch.
  2. October 5Juan Ortiz, our first ambassador, writes to the club for Chicago Adaptive Sports and Adapt To Life, about court time and a wheelchair tournament.
  3. The same dayThe Picklr writes back: open play, clinics, tournaments, even fundraisers are all on the table.
  4. NowOpen play with the crew, alongside the club’s own players. This is just the beginning.

About The Picklr

Indoor courts, and room for the crew.

The Picklr runs indoor pickleball clubs across the country, including Mount Prospect. Its team said yes to wheelchair pickleball on its courts, and we’re grateful.
