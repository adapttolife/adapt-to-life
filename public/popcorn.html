Where the money goes

What it takes to put one athlete on that court.

The US Open is in Naples, thirteen hundred miles from our home courts, and it runs the better part of a week. Three things have to be true before an athlete can play it.

  • Equipment

    about $450

    A sport chair is only as good as what it rolls on. Tires wear out on tournament surfaces, and a cracked paddle ends a weekend early. Tires run about $200, a competition paddle about $250.

  • Training

    about $500

    Nobody arrives at a national championship untrained. We share a practice facility with six to ten wheelchair athletes every week, all year. This is the part of your gift that keeps working long after the tournament ends.

  • Travel

    about $2,300

    Airfare into Naples in the spring runs around $500. Six nights of lodging is over $1,200. Entry is $150 to register plus $45 to $50 per event, and accessible ground transport is on top of that.

About $3,500 puts one athlete on that court. Six athletes is $21,000, which is the goal of Send 6 to the US Open. These costs climb every year, so we budget honestly rather than optimistically.

Every bag sends about six dollars to the fund. A set of tires is roughly thirty bags. One athlete is roughly five hundred.

This drive is going for $1,000. Twenty-one of them, or the equivalent in gifts, is six athletes in Naples.

How it works

Three taps, and you are done.

  1. 01

    Open the store

    Each campaign has its own pop-up store link. It stays open for a set number of days, then closes.

  2. 02

    Order what you like

    Twelve flavors, single bags or sets. There is a $22 order minimum, which is about two bags. Double Good ships to your door, or to whoever you are sending it to.

  3. 03

    Half comes back

    Fifty percent of every dollar goes to the fund, and from the fund to the equipment, training, and travel above.

Orders have to go through our pop-up store link for the fund to receive its half. Buying from the regular Double Good shop does not count toward our campaign.

The multiplier

Run a store of your own.

This is the part that decides how much we raise. One store reaches one circle of friends. Ten stores reach ten. Anyone can open their own in a couple of minutes, share the link with their people, and every order still lands in the same fund.
