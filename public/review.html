You picked the mosaic for the front page and the banner language for everything else. Both are applied. The header carries a photograph and five words — no buttons, no meter — because the ask moved to the nav, where it now sits at every width including phones, immediately left of the hamburger.

Iteration: the header, sitewide · staging only · updated 2026-09-08

  1. Home — the mosaicNineteen photographs on a wall, three planes deep, with two haze planes interleaved between the depth layers so the air between them does the separating. The wordmark takes the top-left corner and the shade over it is radial from that corner rather than a full-height column, which is what hands the bottom-left back to the pictures. Panel heights are aspect-ratio, not a percentage derived from a fixed 1440×792 — that bug made the wall open gaps at 1920.
  2. About — the interior bandThe same bottom-aligned columns, the same pitch black, the same orange as a floor bloom, held well back behind the page’s own words. It takes variant 2’s language, not its layout: 2 puts type on the floor, which works for five words and not for an eyebrow, a headline and a sub-line.
  3. The Promise — a taller header on the same bandTwo-line headline, more room, same treatment. Worth checking that the band still reads as texture rather than as a photograph competing with the number.
  4. Donate — the band under a live widgetThe Givebutter embed sits on the band. This page is also why the band exists in a light pg-* asset tier: the first rollout shipped the full banner columns here and put 450KB of decoration on a page with a payment form. check-site caught it on its own-bytes budget.
  5. Volunteer — the generated pageThis one is built by build-volunteer.mjs, so it gets the band from the same shared module the hand-written pages use rather than from a hand edit. The first attempt edited it directly and build-volunteer --check failed — correctly, because the next build would have deleted the change.
  6. Tim — deliberately untouchedTim and Hustle & Heart keep their own two-column photo heroes. A texture band behind a portrait of Tim would be two pictures arguing.

Donate moved into the nav on phones. Left of the hamburger, on every page. It is the same element as the desktop button rather than a second copy — below 860px the nav list stays in flow and every child except the CTA is hidden — so there is one Donate link in the markup sitewide and no chance of the two drifting apart.

Switching the front page is one command. node scripts/apply-hero.mjs 1. It is idempotent, and it reads the same definitions the comparison lab reads, so what got approved at /hero-1 and what ships are the same bytes.

Still open: serif or grotesk. The homepage is set in the site’s serif; five of the nine candidates were grotesk and you have leaned that way twice. Whichever wins should probably run through the whole site rather than living only at the top of one page.
