Staging review

One promise, and five pages that point at it.

This is live. Pushed to adapttolife.org on 4 September 2026. The tour below is what changed; the links now go to the staging copy of the same build.

The site was carrying two promises. The older one lived on /about, said one hundred percent reaches the athlete, never named who covers the overhead, and described athlete grants only. Three pages linked to it as the full statement. There is one now, it covers programs as well as athletes, and it says out loud that Alec pays for the rest.

Iteration: the promise · shipped to production · updated 2026-09-04

  1. The Promise, read this one properlyNew page, 481 words. Almost all of it is definitions, because a claim you cannot check is a slogan. Why we can say one hundred percent: an athlete grant goes straight to the vendor, program support goes straight to the program’s costs, and no dollar stops here. Why we can say zero percent to admin: because the page defines admin before anyone else does. And the section that protects us, saying safety, insurance, waivers and concussion protocol are program costs, with a test so the next obligation is covered without an edit.
  2. Donate, and Ways to Give merged into itTwo pages were answering “how do I give.” The advisor facts moved here, legal name, EIN, determination letter, because a donor giving through a broker or a will needed five facts that sat on a page nobody landed on. The campaign band already showed what Ways to Give showed, and its “what is coming up” section rendered empty. /ways-to-give now 301s here. The “0% goes to admin” stat links to the Promise, because that was an undefined claim sitting beside a Donate button.
  3. Hustle & Heart, said once and by someoneThis page claimed “one hundred percent reaches the athlete” three times: as a headline, as a tail on the grant process, and again in the closing. Once now, where it is the headline. “Overhead is covered separately, on purpose” is gone from the whole site; here it reads “Alec covers the overhead himself, so your gift never has to.”
  4. About, the second promise retiredCut to a claim and a link. It keeps its id="promise" so any link to /about#promise still lands. The one line worth keeping was the one the Promise page does not make: we don’t replace existing programs, we fund the costs their budgets were never built to carry.
  5. Roadmap, kept on purposeThis was meant to fold into the Promise and redirect. Reading it first, the redirect would have deleted the charitable registration status and the commitment to publish annual financials, which is a promise made against the very page it would have vanished into. The two pages do different jobs, so they link instead.

Site-wide: The Promise is in the nav under About and in the footer where Ways to give used to be. Five pages now link into it. “Overhead is covered separately” returns nothing anywhere. /ways-to-give 301s to /donate, its file is deleted rather than left to drift, and the three scripts that generated a share card or a link for it were updated so nothing rebuilds it.

One gap this found in our own tooling: check-site.mjs keeps a hand-maintained page list, and /promise was not on it, so the newest page on the site was invisible to the sweep. The file warns about exactly this, having lost /volunteer the same way for a day. It is on the list now.
