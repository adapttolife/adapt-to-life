Staging review

Option 2, across the whole brand system.

The exact coral in Brand Style Guide Version 2 is now the site's primary accent. This walk shows it in the places visitors actually encounter it, from the homepage and application flow to browser icons and social cards.

Iteration: Option 2 color migration · updated 2026-08-09

  1. HomepageStart with the hero, navigation, buttons, campaign card, event links and footer. The logo gesture and every primary accent now use the exact Option 2 coral.
  2. Application flowCheck coral on a long, light page. Text links use a darker coral derived from Option 2 so small type still clears WCAG contrast on white.
  3. Donation pageCheck the primary calls to action and focus states. The exact coral stays prominent while darker hover and pressed states keep white button labels readable.
  4. Campaign pageThis is the highest concentration of brand accents. Look for one coherent coral rather than the mix of older orange values that was present before.

Also updated, but not separate stops: browser favicons, the Apple home-screen icon, Open Graph sharing cards, QR artwork defaults and the internal admin shell all use Option 2. New cache-safe filenames keep browsers and social networks from serving the retired orange.

Deliberately unchanged: gold stays reserved for Tim's memorial treatment, status colors keep their semantic meanings, and old cache-versioned image files remain byte-identical as historical artifacts. The active site no longer references those files.
