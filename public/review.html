Staging review

The giving page stopped making everyone wait.

Turnstile no longer loads on pages nobody submits from, and the Givebutter embed no longer parses nine megabytes at every visitor who merely lands on /donate. Nothing moved on the page. The test is that everything still works, and that the giving form still takes a gift.

Iteration: Turnstile and Givebutter load only when they are needed · updated 2026-07-25

  1. Donate, on your phoneStill the one that matters. Scroll to the form, tap an amount, then Continue, and confirm you reach the card step. Stop there, do not complete a gift. The form now loads as you approach it rather than at the instant the page opens, and the space it needs is held for it, so the page no longer jumps when it arrives.
  2. Hustle & Heart FundThe second giving form, same change, same test. Scroll down to it and confirm the amounts appear.
  3. HomeScroll all the way to the bottom and use the newsletter field in the footer. That form is protected by Turnstile, which used to load on every page whether or not you ever reached a form. It now loads as you come near one. If the signup still says "You're in", the guard works.
  4. Contact — on the LIVE site, not hereThis link deliberately leaves staging. Staging has no Airtable binding and no Turnstile secret, on purpose, so that it can never touch real data: a contact submission here always fails with "Could not save right now", no matter what the code does. That is staging working correctly, not a bug. The submit path can only be proved on the live site, so send one real test message there and confirm it says thanks. It is the one thing about this iteration that automation cannot verify, because a headless browser never receives a Turnstile token — which was equally true before this change.
  5. WaiverThe compliance page, untouched by design. Its form sits high enough that its protection still loads immediately. Confirm it looks and behaves exactly as before.

What moved, in numbers. The home page went from 1366KB over 31 requests to 552KB over 20, because Turnstile was about 60% of it and nobody had asked for it yet. On /donate, the page now finishes loading in 2.6 seconds instead of 14.1, and the main thread is blocked for 72ms instead of 2062ms. Layout shift on a phone went from as high as 0.76 to a flat zero.

What did not move, honestly. Once you actually engage the giving panel, Givebutter still pulls about 8.5MB of Stripe, Google Maps and its own bundles, and there is still a busy moment of roughly half a second while that parses. That is their code and it cannot be trimmed. What changed is who pays for it: before, every visitor who merely landed on /donate paid it, whether or not they ever intended to give. Now it is only spent on someone heading for the form. Removing it entirely would mean hiding the amounts behind a click, which is a decision about donations rather than about speed, so I have not made it.

One open item. On a desktop browser, /donate still shifts slightly on about half of loads. It is not the giving form (reserving its exact height changed nothing), not the reveal animation (it happens with animation off) and not only the webfonts (it happens with them blocked). It is measured, narrowed and written down rather than guessed at, and it does not affect phones at all.

The share cards already shipped. The previous tour on this page walked the six link-preview cards; those merged and went live while this work was in flight, so this page has moved on to the current iteration. Nothing about them changed here.
