The Road to the US Open.

The road is a season of tournament travel for Chicago Pickleball: Wheelie Big Dills, ending at the US Open in Naples in spring 2027. The US Open picks players by lottery, so nothing is guaranteed.

Every gift goes to the Hustle & Heart Fund for Chicago Pickleball: Wheelie Big Dills, every week first, then the road to the US Open. Money raised earlier for the US Open, the popcorn drive included, keeps its terms and stays on the road. How Giving Works.

Why the road

The money goes where the matches are.

Many tournaments give athletes more matches for far less than the US Open. A tournament trip runs about $1,000 an athlete; one athlete’s US Open has cost about $3,500 the last couple of years. Funding a season, not one trip, reaches more of the crew.

Two of the athletes we support know the way: Robby Wolf has played three straight US Opens, and Brian Liss has won silver and bronze there.

It takes skill and a little luck to get to the US Open. It shouldn’t take money.

If an athlete’s name comes up, money shouldn’t be the reason they stay home. We want the best of the best in Naples.

Where the money goes

What the road pays for.

$21,000 for the season: about 21 tournament trips at about $1,000 each. A trip is one athlete at one tournament.

  • Entry fees

    Registration and event fees, set by each tournament, the US Open included.

  • Travel

    Gas or flights, from Chicago to the courts.

  • Accessible rooms

    A room near the courts that works for a wheelchair.

Individual needs and costs will vary. We pay vendors directly, or reimburse approved expenses.
