For Selkirk Sport

Let’s grow the game we play together.

In pickleball, a player in a wheelchair and a standing player can share a court, and even a team. Adapt To Life has started making lessons for that game. We have a national champion coach, and a crew of wheelchair players who bring home medals.

We’re not just asking for money. We’d like a partnership that’s good for both of us.

02 · The bigger game

The biggest audience for this game is standing up.

In hybrid doubles, each team has one player in a wheelchair and one standing. The US Open and the USA Pickleball National Championships both run hybrid divisions. Our own Pete Petersen and Alec Tranel won hybrid silver at the 2025 US Open, and every adaptive night at our clubs puts chairs on the courts beside the clubs’ own members.

Those standing players are Selkirk TV’s audience. What Selkirk TV hasn’t had is a lesson on playing with, and against, a player in a chair: where to stand, which balls to take, and how one shot sets up a partner. We looked through close to fifty of its courses and couldn’t find one.

That’s where we see the biggest reach and the biggest impact: not only building wheelchair pickleball, but building the sport together.

“Hybrid is, I would say, probably one of the most important divisions for us, because it really emphasizes the inclusivity of playing with a standing player.”

Ryan Anthony, our coach, in the Marshall Independent, 2024

What we’ve made

Nine lessons, made this month.

The hybrid lesson above, four more just below, and four from the wheelchair game. Each one is short and made for a phone: the court drawn to scale, every shot slowed down and explained, with narration and captions built in. We keep improving them as players and coaches tell us what works.

Tap to play, with sound. Captions are on screen, and the narration is a synthetic voice. Each lesson draws on USA Pickleball’s rules and on coaching from pros, Selkirk’s own included, and the teaching guide behind each one notes what changes for the chair.

03 · What we’d build with Selkirk

A library for the whole game, presented by Selkirk.

Selkirk TV and Selkirk Academy teach the standing game. We’d like to add the game we play together, one series at a time, ready for your app, your camps and your coaches.

  • Series 1

    Play together

    For standing players with a partner, or an opponent, in a chair.

    1. Your return sets up your partner Made
    2. See the angles from the next contact Made
    3. Use the drop to move as a team Made
    4. Cover the ball and give your partner time Made
    5. Poach with a plan Made
    6. Make a plan before the point
    7. Stay connected without crowding
  • Series 2

    The wheelchair game

    For players in a chair, from first games to tournaments.

    1. Ocean and Ponds Made
    2. Read your opponent, build the point Made
    3. Return deep, cover the reply Made
    4. The third shot drop Made
    5. Push, turn and recover with a paddle in hand
    6. When to use the second bounce
  • Series 3

    Coach it

    For coaches, club pros and volunteers.

    1. Run a court where chairs and standing players mix
    2. Feeding drills for chair movement
    3. Adapt a lesson to the player in front of you
    4. Start an adaptive night at your club
    5. Add wheelchair and hybrid divisions to a tournament

Where the crew plays and builds

Three clubs, and the programs around them.

The bigger part of the story is the year leading up to the US Open: training and travel all season, building the best wheelchair pickleball in the Chicago area, and helping other clubs and organizations start adaptive programs.

  • Des Plaines
  • Lake Forest
  • Mount Prospect

How it works

Simple terms, kept in writing.

  • Selkirk would be the only paddle brand Adapt To Life names as a crew sponsor for the term.
  • Our posts thank you by name, logo and link. Anything more, like an athlete promoting a paddle, is between Selkirk and that athlete.
  • In the six new lessons in step one, the players use Selkirk paddles and each episode names Selkirk. That’s recognition of the sponsorship, not a review or endorsement by Adapt To Life or any athlete. Selkirk’s name stays on those six lessons for as long as we show them. “Presented by” on a series as a whole runs one year at a time, like every other part.
  • Crediting Ryan Anthony’s coaching isn’t an endorsement of any paddle, and nothing in this partnership asks him to use or promote Selkirk gear. His own equipment stays between him and the brands he works with.
  • Adapt To Life funds and supports the crew. Clubs host the play, and coaches and ambassadors lead it.
  • A short recap every month with attendance, views and results, and at the end of the season or year, what your money paid for.
  • The US Open picks players by lottery, so no one knows ahead of time how many names will come up. We raise for it all the same, and what Naples doesn’t use stays on the road.
