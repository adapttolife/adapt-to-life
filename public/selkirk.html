For Selkirk Sport

Let’s build the wheelchair game together.

Chicago Pickleball: Wheelie Big Dills is a crew of wheelchair pickleball players that Adapt To Life funds and supports. In August, Juan Ortiz asked the APP Tour for its first wheelchair division, and it drew more teams than any other amateur bracket there. In October, the crew came home from Monster Smash with six medals.

We’re just getting started, and we’d like Selkirk with us for what comes next.

Already in your family

Three Locos. Four medals.

Juan Ortiz, Bob Swanson and Robby Wolf play the Bread & Butter Loco, and they took their Locos to Monster Smash. Juan is a Bread & Butter ambassador. What comes next

Juan asked the APP Tour for a wheelchair division at its Chicago stop. Five full teams signed up.

Selkirk sponsors tournaments across the country. Let’s ask their directors for wheelchair divisions, together.

Where the crew plays and builds

Three clubs, and the programs around them.

The bigger part of the story is the year leading up to the US Open: training and travel all season, building the best wheelchair pickleball in the Chicago area, and helping other clubs and organizations start adaptive programs.

  • Des Plaines
  • Lake Forest
  • Mount Prospect
  • 10 to 15 athletes a night at three Open Court clubs, playing beside the clubs’ own members.
  • Our very own Pete Petersen, who played hybrid doubles at the US Open in 2025, coaches GLASA’s weekly wheelchair pickleball night at Brick House.
  • Clinics are scheduled with ACE, The Picklr and the Lincolnway Special Recreation Association.
  • Coaching that helped the Wisconsin Adaptive Sports Association start pickleball, and a partnership with Chicago Adaptive Sports, where Juan serves on the board.
  • Juan found the game at a Chicago Park District clinic in 2025. This fall he helped the Park District start a free adaptive pickleball program, four weeks at McFetridge Sports Center.

Grow together

Start with gear. Grow one season at a time.

Step 1

Gear up

This winter, through the US Open in April

Selkirk brings

  • New paddles for the athletes we support.
  • Growth Program paddles for new players at Open Court clubs.
  • Gear for a fundraiser night at a club.
  • If you’d like to add money now: $1,000 is about what one tournament trip costs for one athlete.

Selkirk gets

  • A thank you across our social channels, and your name on our supporters wall.
  • Your name and logo on the thank you sign at our table when the crew is at a club event, a clinic or a tournament, where the host allows it.
  • Photos of your gear in use, in our own thank you posts.
  • A short recap every month through Naples, so step two is decided on real numbers.

Sponsorships go to the Hustle & Heart Fund. If your name goes on it, your money pays for it. Each part carries one sponsor’s name at a time, for its year or season, held by the first sponsor to sign. How it works

Simple terms, kept in writing.

  • Selkirk would be the only paddle brand Adapt To Life names as a crew sponsor for the term.
  • Our posts thank you by name, logo and link. Anything more, like an athlete promoting a paddle, is between Selkirk and that athlete.
  • Adapt To Life funds and supports the crew. Clubs host the play, and coaches and ambassadors lead it.
  • A short recap every month with attendance, views and results, and at the end of the season or year, what your money paid for.
  • The US Open picks players by lottery, so no one knows ahead of time how many names will come up. We raise for it all the same, and what Naples doesn’t use stays on the road.
The ask

Start this winter. Grow from there.

We’d like to start with step one this winter. If Selkirk wants its name on the road to Naples, it’s open now. If you’d rather watch this season play out first, let’s talk about the next one after the US Open.

Better yet, come play a game from a sport chair with the crew. We’ll save you a chair.
