The campaign

Send 6 to the US Open Spring 2027.

Help six adaptive pickleball athletes prepare for the US Open in Naples in spring 2027. We are raising $21,000 for equipment, training, and travel.

$21,000 to go

For new gifts: if Send 6 is fully funded or cannot go ahead, remaining campaign funds from those gifts will support the Hustle & Heart Fund. How Giving Works.

The goal, in people

Six seats. One athlete each.

Our fundraising target is $3,500 per athlete. Together, the six seats make up the $21,000 campaign goal.

    Juan Ortiz holds the first seat and is leading the campaign. The other athletes are selected through the fund’s grant process, and we will name them here once they are confirmed.

    Where the money goes

    What it takes to put one athlete on that court.

    Playing at the US Open takes preparation as well as a trip to Naples. The campaign brings those needs together: equipment, training, and travel.

    • Equipment

      A sport chair is only as good as what it rolls on. Tires wear out on tournament surfaces, and a cracked paddle ends a weekend early.

    • Training

      The work starts long before Naples. Our athletes train every week at Brick House Pickleball, our home court and first partner.

    • Travel

      A flight, an accessible place to stay, a way to get from the hotel to the court. Entry fees and meals, too.

    We're raising $21,000 toward equipment, training, and travel for six athletes. The target is $3,500 per athlete; individual needs and costs will vary.
