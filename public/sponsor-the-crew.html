Sponsor Chicago wheelchair pickleball

Build it with us.

Chicago Pickleball: Wheelie Big Dills is a crew of wheelchair pickleball players growing the sport across the Chicago area. Adapt To Life is proud to fund and support them, and sponsors and partners make every level possible.

See the packages Email us
What we fund

An ecosystem for adaptive pickleball.

Sport is health, independence and community. For people who use wheelchairs, the hardest part is often finding a court, a group and a way to afford it. We are building all three across the Chicago area, with clubs, organizations and groups. Going to the US Open is one goal inside it.

  1. Play anytimeOpen play and drop ins with clubs, organizations and groups, on different days across the week.
  2. Train to competeMemberships for athletes who train regularly so they can travel to tournaments.
  3. CompeteMonthly tournaments, the Chicago Classic, presented by Adapt To Life, and the road beyond.
  4. Your name on itSponsors and partners fund each level, and see the results every month.

Ways to sponsor

Pick your place on the court.

Every sponsorship goes to the Hustle & Heart Fund at Adapt To Life, a 501(c)(3), and is tax deductible to the extent allowed by law. Ambassadors like Juan show us where it will do the most, and we pay programs and vendors directly.

Level 1 · Play anytime

Open play and drop ins with clubs, organizations and groups across the week. At ACE, a drop-in is $15, and Adapt To Life covers it so every athlete plays free.

Night sponsor

Sponsor a night

$225 a night

Court time for up to 15 athletes for a night at ACE, at $15 each. Your business named on that night’s posts. A perfect fit for a local shop, practice or restaurant.

Clubs, organizations and coaches

Build a program

In kind

We help clubs, organizations and coaches build and manage adaptive programs: funding, coaching know-how, players and content. Your program, our support.

Level 2 · Train to compete

Two of the athletes we support, Robby and Brian, have each played the US Open the last three years. Memberships help more athletes train for that level, because it takes more than one session a week: an unlimited ACE premium membership, $129 a month. Sponsors cover the membership, so athletes still play free.

Presenting sponsor

Train to Compete, presented by you

$15,480 a year

Ten athletes on unlimited memberships for a year. Your name on Train to Compete, every Train to Compete post and every athlete spotlight.

Athlete sponsor

Sponsor an athlete

$1,548 a year

One athlete’s unlimited membership for a year, $129 a month. Your name on that athlete’s spotlights, and a note from them at the end of the season.
