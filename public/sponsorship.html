How we partner

The Open Court Model.

Every athlete deserves an open court: a place to train, compete and belong. We open them with clubs, YMCAs, schools and companies, and no host has to give its courts away to be part of it.

  1. The host
    Brings
    Courts on a regular night, at a club, YMCA, school or park.
    Gets
    Paid court time, new players and their families, and a story that sets the club apart.
  2. The athlete
    Brings
    Their game, led by ambassadors like Juan Ortiz, with coaching and volunteers from us.
    Gets
    Open courts to train, compete and bring the next athlete in.
  3. The supporter
    Brings
    Funding, planned at the host’s regular rates.
    Gets
    Results they can see and numbers that add up. See the numbers
  4. A program that lasts

    Run by the host and backed by all of us, season after season.

Open Court clubs today ACE Pickleball Club in Des Plaines, Brick House Pickleball in Lake Forest and The Picklr in Mount Prospect.

What partnership makes possible

Play. Train. Compete.

Partner support goes to the Hustle & Heart Fund. We pay clubs and vendors directly. Right now the fund builds three levels of wheelchair pickleball across the Chicago area, and each level pays for something different.

  1. Play anytimeCourt time, so athletes can drop in and play free.$225 covers up to 15 athletes
  2. Train to competeMemberships for athletes who train for tournaments, because one session a week is not enough.$129 a month an athlete, $1,548 a year
  3. CompeteEntry fees, travel and tournaments, including the Chicago Classic.From $500 a tournament
