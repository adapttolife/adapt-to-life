Where your support goes

Equipment, training, and travel. Right now, the road to Naples.

Partner support generally goes to the Hustle & Heart Fund, then to a campaign. Right now, that campaign is Send 6 to the US Open.

  1. Train every weekAt Brick House, all four seasons.
  2. Play tournamentsMatch play you cannot get in practice.
  3. TravelEntry fees, flights, and a place to stay, to earn a ranking.
  4. The US OpenNaples, spring 2027. Our goal is to send six.
