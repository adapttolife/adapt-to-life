Juan Ortiz, Bob Swanson and Robby Wolf play the Bread & Butter Loco, and they took their Locos to Monster Smash. Juan is a Bread & Butter ambassador. His code has earned $350.34 in commission on Bread & Butter orders, as of November 13, 2026. When Bread & Butter pays it out, Juan gives it to the Hustle & Heart Fund.

How we partner

The Open Court Model.

Every athlete deserves an open court: a place to train, compete and belong. We open them with clubs, YMCAs, schools and companies, and no host has to give its courts away to be part of it.

  1. The host
    Brings
    Courts on a regular night, at a club, YMCA, school or park.
    Gets
    Paid court time, new players and their families, and a story that sets the club apart.
  2. The athlete
    Brings
    Their game, led by ambassadors like Juan Ortiz, with coaching and volunteers from us.
    Gets
    Open courts to train, compete and bring the next athlete in.
  3. The supporter
    Brings
    Funding, planned at the host’s regular rates.
    Gets
    Results they can see and numbers that add up. See the numbers
  4. A program that lasts

    Run by the host and backed by all of us, season after season.

Open Court clubs today ACE Pickleball Club in Des Plaines, Brick House Pickleball in Lake Forest and The Picklr in Mount Prospect.

What partnership makes possible

Play. Train. Compete.

Partner support goes to the Hustle & Heart Fund, and we pay clubs and vendors directly or reimburse approved expenses. Right now it funds the Wheelie Big Dills at three levels, each one a line in our budget for the year ahead.

  1. Play anytimeCourt time, so athletes can drop in and play free.$225 a night for up to 15 athletes
  2. Train to competeMemberships for athletes who train for tournaments, because one session a week is not enough.$1,548 a year for one athlete
  3. Compete on the roadEntry fees, travel and accessible rooms for the crew, through the 2027 US Open. Gifts past the road go toward the Chicago Classic, presented by Adapt To Life.$21,000 for the season

Sponsorships go to the Hustle & Heart Fund. If your name goes on it, your money pays for it: Captain pays for court time, Train to Compete for ten memberships, Champion for the road and a Founding Partner for the whole year ahead. Each part carries one sponsor’s name at a time, for its year or season, held by the first sponsor to sign. If a part is already paid for, in full or in part, the money it doesn’t need goes to the rest of the goal, and we tell you where before you give. Other sponsorships and gifts fill every week first, then the road to the US Open. The US Open picks players by lottery.
