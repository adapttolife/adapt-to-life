01 · A note from Alec Tranel

Tom and Mitch, thank you for Cincinnati.

You made 2026 entry free for wheelchair players and helped with travel and meals. Six athletes we support from Chicago Pickleball: Wheelie Big Dills made the trip from Chicago and came home talking about The Pickle Lodge, your volunteers, and the friends they made at Lunken Landing.

What you built at Monster Smash is the exact standard adaptive pickleball needs. I want to partner with you as an ambassador for Spooky Spokes, help grow the sport alongside your team, and put a real spotlight on every player who shows up.

Alec TranelFounder and Executive Director, Adapt To Life

02 · Grow hybrid pickleball

Grow hybrid play from Sundays to center court.

You are already pairing wheelchair players and standing Lodge members on Sundays, and we both know hybrid has room to grow much bigger. Pete Petersen and I won hybrid silver at the 2025 US Open, and at our Chicago Open Court clubs we see how quickly standing members get hooked once they learn the rhythm and share a court.

Wheelchair player Standing player Return shot

The standing player may use one bounce.

Coach Ryan Anthony Adapt To Life Coach · PPR Certified

“Hybrid is, I would say, probably one of the most important divisions for us, because it really emphasizes the inclusivity of playing with a standing player.”

Ryan Anthony, Adapt To Life Coach · Marshall Independent, 2024

Coach Ryan Anthony is a PPR-certified coach, 2024 USA Pickleball wheelchair doubles national champion, and 2024 and 2025 US Open wheelchair doubles silver medalist. His coaching supplies our 35-lesson library; an in-person clinic at Lunken Landing would be a separate invitation if you ever want one.

05 · The athlete package

A spotlight for every athlete who wants one.

In Cincinnati we made custom result cards for the six Chicago athletes we support and filmed moments on court and at the medal presentations. Brian Liss got a card that told his story as a competitor. That same invitation should go to everyone who plays at Spooky Spokes.

I want to work alongside Tom, Mitch, and The Pickle Lodge’s marketing team as an ambassador and marketing partner. Together we can offer every athlete who feels comfortable being featured a personal result card, a court clip, and a short story they can share, then take those real stories to Cincinnati businesses, regional partners, and national sponsors so Spooky Spokes sets the year-round standard for growing adaptive pickleball.
