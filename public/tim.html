In memory

Tim Gruensfelder

All hustle. All heart. The athlete who set the standard for how the game should be played.

1996–2018

His story

The kid who never quit.

Tim Gruensfelder was a competitor from the start. Cancer as a kid changed his body; it never touched what drove him.

He found wheelchair basketball, and on the court he found what the sport gives you when everything else is stripped away: an identity, a community, a reason to show up. He wasn't the most talented player in the gym. He was the hardest-working one. Every loose ball, every sprint, every drill.

He earned a college scholarship, proof that adaptive sport is a pathway, not a pastime. In 2018 the cancer came back. Tim was 22.

His standard

All hustle. All heart.

His legacy

What Tim left behind.

After Tim passed, his family kept him on the court. His father Bob and the Gruensfelder family created the Timmy G Classic, an annual wheelchair basketball tournament that carries his name forward.

When we built the Hustle & Heart Fund, naming it after anyone else never crossed our minds. Tim showed us that the difference between playing and watching is often just money, and that heart was never the thing in short supply.

The fund exists because Tim showed us what happens when an athlete gets the chance to play.
Carry it forward

Put the next athlete on the court.

Every dollar given to the Hustle & Heart Fund puts another athlete on the court, in Tim's name.
