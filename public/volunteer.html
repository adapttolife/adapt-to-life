Before you scroll

What you would be joining.

Built around participation

A recognized 501(c)(3) helping people find adaptive sports and get support to take part.

A scope, not a shift

Every role has a defined job and a person to work with. If we cannot say what done looks like, we will not ask for your time.

Flexible contributions

Remote roles can often fit around your schedule. Event and athlete-facing work has agreed times and requirements.

You hear back either way

A person reads every one. If there is no fit now, we say so.

Fundraising

Help fund the next opportunity.

Build support for equipment, training, and travel.

  • Grant writer Find the foundations that fund adaptive sport, and write the applications. A few hours a month→
  • Fundraising lead Build the annual plan: which campaigns, which asks, in what order. A few hours a month→
  • Sponsorship and partnership lead Bring businesses to the table and keep them there. A few hours a month→
  • Corporate matching champion Get Adapt To Life into your employer's matching program. One hour, once→
  • Planned and major giving advisor Donor advised funds, appreciated stock, bequests. As it comes up→
Operations

Help the organization work responsibly.

Clear records, appropriate advice, and thoughtful review keep the work accountable.

  • CPA or tax preparer Our first Form 990, and keeping the exemption airtight. Seasonal→
  • Bookkeeper Monthly reconciliation alongside our treasurer. An hour or two a month→
  • Nonprofit attorney Bylaws, a conflict of interest policy, and charitable registration as we grow. As it comes up→
  • Trademark counsel Review and protect the marks we use on websites and products. A one time project→
  • Insurance and risk Coverage for clinics, tournaments, volunteers, and the athletes we put on a court. As it comes up→
  • Grant reviewer Review funding requests against agreed criteria. A few hours per cycle→
Stories

Help people understand the work.

Share real stories and useful information, with the people involved.

  • Social media manager Turn our work into posts people stop for. A few hours a week→
  • Writer or editor Athlete stories, the newsletter, and the annual impact report. A few hours a month→
  • Photographer or videographer Shoot our clinics and tournaments. A day at a time→
  • Graphic designer Sponsor decks, one pagers, event graphics, apparel. Project by project→
  • Press and media Get athletes in front of reporters, podcasts and local news. As it comes up→
  • Accessibility reviewer Test this site with the tools our athletes actually use, then tell us what breaks. A one time project→
Participation

Support the people taking part.

Coaching, equipment, events, and connections to programs.

  • Adaptive sports coach Run a clinic, coach a session, teach a sport. A season, or one clinic→
  • Event crew Setup, registration, scorekeeping, teardown. A day at a time→
  • Equipment technician Fit, repair and refurbish chairs and adaptive gear. As it comes up→
  • Athlete mentor Be the person a newly injured athlete or their family calls first. An hour here and there→
  • Program scout Find and verify adaptive programs for the directory. An hour a month→
  • Clinical referral partner Hand someone a brochure the way one was handed to Alec. As it comes up→
Connections

Start with what you can offer.

An introduction, a place to gather, or an idea can be a useful start.

  • Make an introduction A company, a foundation, a venue, a coach, a reporter. One email→
  • Host something A tournament, a clinic, a popcorn drive, a birthday fundraiser. A few weeks of planning→
  • Board and advisory The seats that steer this as it grows. A real commitment→
