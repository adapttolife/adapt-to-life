      Standing ways to give

      Open every day of the year.

      Campaigns start and finish. These do not.

      • Give monthly

        A season of coaching is a monthly cost, so monthly money fits it best. Fifteen dollars a month covers a set of racing tires across a year, and it is the difference between hoping we can say yes and knowing we can.

        Set up monthly giving

      • Sponsor

        Businesses back athletes and get their name where the sport actually happens, on the chair and at the tournament. There is a ladder, and the first rung is smaller than you would guess.

        See the sponsorship ladder
      • Donor-advised fund Your sponsor needs two things to send a grant: our legal name and our EIN. Get them →
      • Stock and securities Giving appreciated stock directly can avoid the capital gains you would owe on selling it first. Start one →
      • Bequests and IRA gifts A line in a will, or a beneficiary designation, costs nothing today and keeps the fund going long after. Get the language →
      • Employer matching Plenty of employers double a gift and most people never ask. Send us the form and we complete our side. Send it over →
      • In honor, in memory Give in someone's name and add a dedication as you check out. The fund itself carries a name. Read whose →

      Not every gift is money. Coaching, bookkeeping, writing a grant, fixing a chair, or making one introduction. There is a board of what we actually need. See where you fit →
